WordsPal privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

WordsPal is a small vocabulary tutor for Hebrew speakers learning English, Russian or Italian, run by one person for a group of friends. This page says what the app and its server at app.wordspal.ai collect, why, and what happens to it.

What is collected

  • Your email address, to send you a sign-in code and to keep your account.
  • Your profile: the username, display name, age and languages you enter.
  • Your learning: the words you look up and save, your answers, and the progress and levels worked out from them.
  • Voice recordings, only when you choose to answer by speaking. A recording is sent to the server to check your pronunciation and is not stored.
  • Photos, only when you photograph or pick a word list to import. A photo is kept only until its words have been read, then deleted.

Who processes it

Lookups, answers to check, recordings and photos are sent to Google's Gemini API, which works out translations, checks answers and reads text on WordsPal's behalf. Sign-in emails are sent through Resend. The server and its database run on Amazon Web Services. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for advertising, and the app contains no ads or third-party trackers.

Security

Everything between the app and the server travels over HTTPS. Your session is kept in the phone's secure storage.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account and learning data are kept while you use the app. To delete them, email contact@wordspal.ai from the address you sign in with, and everything tied to your account is removed.

Children

WordsPal is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
