# Language-exchange and learner chat apps: competitive profile (as of October 2026)

Research done 2026-10-19. App Store listings fetched on that date; where a listing shows a version date without a year (for example "Sep 23"), it is the most recent release before 2026-10-19, so almost certainly 2026. Figures older than 2025 are flagged with their year.

## HelloTalk: translate, transliteration, correction, AI, Favorites, users, pricing, revenue, 2024–2026 AI additions

### Takeaway
HelloTalk is still the largest closed network of strangers for language exchange: it claims 70M+ registered users. Its learning tools sit on a long-press menu on each message (Translate, Transliteration, peer Correction, AI Grammar, Favorite). Its AI push since 2023–2025 went mostly into a family of separate apps sold on the same account, such as HelloWords for spaced-repetition vocabulary, AI Grammar and Talksy for AI conversation. The core chat app has no vocabulary-SRS loop of its own: Favorites are a tagged bookmark list, and HelloTalk's own blog points users to an outside flashcard tool.

### Cited Findings
**Chat tools (long-press a message)**
- HelloTalk's blog lists 9 chat tools, all opened by holding down on a message:
  - Translate: into your native language, with the target changeable.
  - Correction: a community member edits your message and adds notes on mistakes, more natural phrasing or grammar rules. Corrections can be saved as favorites and tagged.
  - Speak: text-to-speech pronunciation.
  - Transliteration: for example Pinyin for Chinese, also Korean and Japanese.
  - AI Grammar: "checks and corrects spelling, syntax and verb tenses using AI".
  - Reply, Copy, Favorite and Recall.
  - The post is undated. — [HelloTalk blog, 9 chat tools](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/blog/9-chat-tools-to-level-up-your-language-exchanges)
- Favorite saves messages and learning points, with tags, keyword search and filters by tag or type. The blog does not describe a vocabulary list, flashcards or spaced repetition. Instead it describes Copy as useful "for pairing with a memorization or flashcard tool", which suggests the core app has none built in. — [HelloTalk blog, 9 chat tools](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/blog/9-chat-tools-to-level-up-your-language-exchanges)
- How peer correction works: long-press a chat message or Moments post, select "Correction", and you land on a "Correct Sentences" page. — [HelloTalk FAQ](https://www.hellotalk.com/ms/faq/general/1734)

**Word-level translation**
- Sources conflict on whether you can translate a single word:
  - Lingopie's review (updated 17 Nov 2025) calls a "word-click tool, which translates a tapped word" HelloTalk's most practical learning tool. It notes the tool cannot cope with a partner's spelling mistakes. — [Lingopie HelloTalk review](https://lingopie.com/blog/hellotalk-review/)
  - HelloTalk's own pages describe translation of whole messages and posts. Holding the profile "Self-introduction" shows meanings of individual phrases and characters. — [HelloTalk blog, translation](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/blog/language-translation-only-deeper)

**AI correction**
- HelloTalk's 2025 Spanish guide claims an "AI correction engine" trained on "tens of millions of real correction samples per day". It says the engine flags tense and subject-verb agreement errors and gives explanations and example sentences. This is self-reported marketing. — [HelloTalk 2025 Spanish guide](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/blog/2025-spanish-learning-guide-best-5-free-apps)
- Lingopie (Nov 2025) reports that a correction on a Moments post can be an AI-generated suggestion sent with one click. It says this "defeats the purpose" of human feedback, because you cannot tell whether a native speaker or the AI wrote it. It also mentions built-in AI grammar help and an AI chatbot in the premium tier. — [Lingopie HelloTalk review](https://lingopie.com/blog/hellotalk-review/)

**The AI app family**
- HelloTalk's "AI apps" page says one HelloTalk account gives access to these apps, with no prices on the page:
  - HelloWords: spaced-repetition vocabulary, "2M+ learners".
  - AI Grammar: real-time writing suggestions and analysis of error patterns, "1.5M+ writers".
  - Talksy: AI role-play conversation partners with speech assessment, "5M+ conversations daily".
  - Wordia: etymology, "800K+".
  - LanguageClass: courses, "1.2M+ course completions".
  - SpeakUp AI, Langbeats and Capsu are also mentioned.
  - The headline says "26 Language Apps". — [HelloTalk AI apps](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/ai-apps)
- Talksy is a separate App Store app ("© 2025 Talksy") from HelloTalk's company. It covers about 10 languages and has Pro tiers: monthly, 3-month, yearly and lifetime. — [App Store: Talksy](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/talksy-ai-language-learning/id6450812140)

**App Store listing and pricing**
- US App Store listing:
  - The description highlights translation, transliteration, instant captions, matching by native language and proficiency (with age, location and gender filters), Voicerooms, livestreams and the Moments feed, in "260+" languages.
  - Rating: 4.6 from about 44K ratings.
  - VIP: $12.99 a month or $79.99 a year, plus an unlabelled $79.99 "VIP Membership". "HT Coins" cost $0.29 to $149.99.
  - Latest version 6.4.20, dated Sep 23 (presumably 2026), is a bug-fix release. — [App Store: HelloTalk](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hellotalk-language-learning/id557130558)
- Lifetime VIP was $149.99 as of late 2022, per FluentU. — [FluentU HelloTalk review](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/hellotalk)

**Free-tier limits**
- Official FAQ: free members get 10 translations a day, across chat, Moments and everything else; VIP is unlimited. — [HelloTalk FAQ "Why can I only use translation 10 times a day?"](https://www.hellotalk.com/ja/faq/hellotalk-basics/265)
- Older third-party figures conflict with that:
  - ActualFluency says free users get 5 translations a day and can start chats with new people 8 times a day. — [ActualFluency](https://actualfluency.com/hellotalk-review)
  - MacTrast says free users get 10 new-partner requests a day against 25 for VIP, and that VIP adds gender search and removes ads, for new US users. — [MacTrast](https://www.mactrast.com/?p=168083)

**Users, scale and revenue**
- Users claimed on the About page:
  - "70M+" users, "70+ million users across 200+ countries", 260+ languages, "1B+ Messages Daily".
  - The timeline says 1M users in 2015 and 30M in 2020. — [HelloTalk About](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/about)
  - The Russian About page says "60+ million". — [HelloTalk About (RU)](https://www.hellotalk.com/ru/about)
- Active-user claims conflict: the AI-apps page says "8M+ active users" site-wide, while a HelloTalk features page says "40M+ active learners". — [HelloTalk AI apps](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/ai-apps); [HelloTalk features page](https://www.hellotalk.com/hi/features/language-ai-apps)
- Revenue:
  - In 2023, founder Zackery Ngai said HelloTalk had over 1M paid users and earned "several million CNY" a month. This is a 2023 figure. — [Wikipedia: HelloTalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HelloTalk)
  - A third-party estimate is far lower: AppGoblin (data updated around early 2026) puts the iOS app at about 678.8K MAU and more than $50K a month in in-app revenue. It is an order of magnitude below the founder's claim, so treat it as a rough estimate. — [AppGoblin: HelloTalk](https://appgoblin.info/apps/557130558)

### Inferences
- HelloTalk's AI strategy is to keep the human network in the core app and send AI to satellite apps. Translation, correction and saving inside chat are not connected to a review loop in the same product: HelloWords has SRS, but the chat app's Favorites are bookmarks. A product where a word tapped in chat lands straight in an SRS queue would beat HelloTalk on the "chat becomes learning" axis.
- AI corrections passed off as peer corrections (Lingopie) suggest users value knowing whether a human or the AI corrected them. A product should label the source of each correction clearly.
- The free translation cap (10 a day) is HelloTalk's main paywall lever. An integrated product with generous word-level lookups would compete directly on the feature HelloTalk rations.

### Gaps
- No audited or company-reported 2024–2026 revenue was found. Funding history was not found in this pass.
- No primary source confirms word-level tap-to-translate inside chat messages: Lingopie says it exists, HelloTalk's own docs describe whole messages. It needs an in-app check.
- No official dated changelog of HelloTalk AI features for 2024–2026. Third-party "2026 feature comparison" sites (app-tatsujin, Univext) gave conflicting claims (for example "500 translations a month", "Passport" matching) and were not used.

## Tandem: translate in chat, corrections, Tandem Pro, AI features, user counts, external-messenger integration

### Takeaway
Tandem (Berlin) is the number-two closed exchange network. It shows the same pattern as HelloTalk: in-chat translation and peer correction, limited translations on free, unlimited on Tandem Pro. It added AI drafting aids in a "next-gen chat": Word Finder, Grammar Check and Inspire. No source shows any integration with WhatsApp, Telegram or other messengers. Its last public user count is 10M+ members (2020); an undated third-party profile says 29M.

### Cited Findings
**Features**
- US App Store listing:
  - Translation "helps you compose a message or translate an incoming one". Message correction and comment features. Voice notes, audio and video calls, group audio "Parties", plus Nearby and Travel.
  - "Millions of Tandem members", 300+ languages and 12 sign languages.
  - Rating 4.6 from 38K ratings. The listing does not mention AI tools or saved words. — [App Store: Tandem](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tandem-conversation-exchange/id959001619)
- Tandem's homepage lists four features (chat, video, correction, translate), claims "millions" of partners and "Over 100,000 5-star reviews". It says nothing about AI, vocabulary saving or messenger integration. — [tandem.net](https://tandem.net)

  - The release notes are undated in the search excerpts, and there is no official launch announcement. — [APKMirror Tandem 6.1.0](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/tripod-technology-gmbh/tandem-language-exchange-speak-any-language/tandem-language-exchange-6-1-0-release/); [APKMirror Tandem 6.29.0](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/tripod-technology-gmbh/tandem-language-exchange-speak-any-language/tandem-language-exchange-6-29-0-release/)

### Gaps
- No official 2024–2026 member count and no revenue figure.
- No official Tandem help-centre page on Pro features or pricing was retrievable. Toolradar's figures are unverified.

## Other apps: Speaky, HiNative, Slowly, Lingbe, Bilingua, Interpals, ConversationExchange, human/AI hybrids (Langotalk, LanguaTalk), Amikumu, Polyglot Club, newcomers

### Takeaway
The long tail splits four ways:
- Smaller stranger networks: Speaky, Interpals, Amikumu, Lingbe.
- Asynchronous formats: Slowly's delayed letters; HiNative's Q&A and correction.
- Web directories that hand the chat off to Skype or WhatsApp: ConversationExchange, Polyglot Club.
- AI-only "chat" tutors: Langotalk, Talksy, Speaky's AI Partners.

None combines human chat in the user's existing messenger with word-level lookup and review.

## Do any let users bring EXISTING contacts / chats from WhatsApp or Telegram, or are they all closed networks of strangers?

### Takeaway
Every exchange app profiled is a closed network built on matching strangers. None imports WhatsApp or Telegram contacts or chats, and none works inside those messengers. The only movement toward external messengers runs the other way: web directories (ConversationExchange, Telegram exchange hubs) hand users off to WhatsApp, Skype or Telegram, after which learning tools stop.

Inside Telegram and WhatsApp, what exists is AI tutor bots (Poly, Behappy Speak) and translate-and-save bots (LingoCop). None found adds translation and correction to your human conversations with your existing contacts. The closest precedent is the 2015 iOS app Hiway, which let you chat with your own friends and tap words into an SRS deck, but inside its own app. The "existing contacts in your existing messenger" position looks open.

### Cited Findings
- **HelloTalk** matches by native language and proficiency, with age, location and gender filters. The listing describes no contact import or external-messenger integration. — [App Store: HelloTalk](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hellotalk-language-learning/id557130558)
- **Tandem's** homepage and App Store listing describe in-app chat, calls, Nearby and Travel discovery. No WhatsApp or Telegram integration is mentioned. — [tandem.net](https://tandem.net); [App Store: Tandem](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tandem-conversation-exchange/id959001619)
- **ConversationExchange** works by email, and contact extends to Skype or WhatsApp, so the learning context is lost once users move. — [FluentU pen-pal guide](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/learn/foreign-language-penpal/)
- **Telegram exchange hubs** are directories of partners. The conversations then happen in plain Telegram. — [language.exchange: Telegram](https://en.language.exchange/sns/Telegram/)
- **Telegram AI tutor bots** (bot-to-learner, not learner-to-human):
  - Poly: voice messages, with corrections and tips after each message. Vendor claims. — [2-linguo / Poly](https://2-linguo.duckdns.org/en/)
  - Behappy Speak: English and German scenario practice that remembers your mistakes. — [Hunted.space: Behappy Speak](https://hunted.space/product/behappy-speak)
- **LingoCop** is a Telegram translator that saves each translation to a vocabulary list and sends flashcard reminders on days 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30. It is a translation bot, not a chat overlay. — [LingoCop (Notion)](https://lingocop.notion.site/Learn-foreign-words-on-autopilot-with-bite-sized-practice-94bb794d70c6492f8e94807d8f6ff3e8)
- **WhatsApp:** search found only generic chatbot templates. — [Makerobos WhatsApp template](https://chatman.makerobos.com/pages/whatsapp-template-language-learning-chatbot)
  - A product called "Learn A Language With Friends", reported as using WhatsApp for vocabulary and grammar practice, could not be fetched (HTTP 403), so how it works and whether it is live are unverified. — [TrendHunter](https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/learn-a-language)
- **Hiway (iOS, 2015):** "Chat with your friends, click on words, and add them to your learning deck" with spaced-repetition flashcards, plus verified teachers. English–Chinese focus. Whether it is live is unknown. — [Hacking Chinese: Hiway (2015)](https://challenges.hackingchinese.com/resources/stories/379-hiway-english-chinese-language-exchange-community)

### Inferences
- The differentiator stands up against this sample: an overlay or companion that works on top of WhatsApp or Telegram with people the user already knows avoids the hardest problems of exchange apps. Those problems are the cold start of finding a partner, flaky strangers, and dating behaviour (see the complaints section).
- The trade-off is that incumbents own both the conversation and the data. A messenger integration has to handle access to message content (platform APIs and privacy), which no competitor has had to solve. This research did not check the technical or policy feasibility.
- Hiway's existence (2015) and its unknown status suggest friend-based chat-and-learn was tried as a standalone app. An unconfirmed hypothesis: making people move their friends to a new chat app is the failure mode that integrating with existing messengers avoids.

### Gaps
- Exhaustive confirmation that no app integrates with WhatsApp or Telegram is not possible from web search alone. Keyboards (for example translate-and-correct keyboards) and the messengers' own built-in translation were out of scope here and may be covered by other researchers.
- Hiway's current status and its user numbers were not found.

## Is word-level tap-to-translate offered anywhere, and is it linked to a vocabulary list / spaced repetition?

### Takeaway
In the human-exchange incumbents, translation is mainly whole-message on long-press. HelloTalk may offer a word-click lookup (reported by Lingopie in 2025; HelloTalk's own docs are not clear), but it is not wired to SRS. Favorites are bookmarks, and HelloTalk sells SRS separately as HelloWords. Word-tap linked to a vocabulary or SRS list does exist, but mostly in AI-only chat apps (Langotalk, "Language Learning AI Chat", Talkable.chat), in Telegram translation bots (LingoCop), and in the old Hiway app. No live human-to-human messenger product was found that does tap a word, then save it, then review it with spaced repetition.

### Cited Findings
- HelloTalk: a "word-click tool, which translates a tapped word", per Lingopie (Nov 2025). HelloTalk's own blog describes Translate as applying to a message or post. — [Lingopie](https://lingopie.com/blog/hellotalk-review/); [HelloTalk blog, 9 chat tools](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/blog/9-chat-tools-to-level-up-your-language-exchanges)
- HelloTalk Favorites hold tagged, searchable messages and corrections, with no SRS described. HelloWords, a separate app on the same account, provides "spaced repetition". — [HelloTalk blog, 9 chat tools](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/blog/9-chat-tools-to-level-up-your-language-exchanges); [HelloTalk AI apps](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/ai-apps)
- Langotalk (AI tutor): v7.1.0 lets users "click key words and phrases from chats", and v7.0.0 added spaced repetition for vocabulary. — [App Store: Langotalk](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6449473489)
- "Language Learning AI Chat" (AI chat): tap any word or phrase for an instant translation, with review material generated from past conversations. — [MWM listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/language-learning-ai-chat/6754209933)
- Talkable.chat (iPhone): saves words from a chat, show or song into a personal vocabulary list, then generates AI practice conversations from it. — [App Store id6748417362](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6748417362)
- Flipchat translates incoming chat messages into the language being learned. Tap a message to see the translation, swipe to hear it. Whether it saves words is not stated. — [Neura: Flipchat](https://www.neura.market/ai-tools-directory/language-learning-edu/flipchat)
- LingoCop (Telegram): each translation is saved to a vocabulary list, with SRS reminders on days 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30. — [LingoCop](https://lingocop.notion.site/Learn-foreign-words-on-autopilot-with-bite-sized-practice-94bb794d70c6492f8e94807d8f6ff3e8)
- Hiway (2015): tap words in chats with friends and add them to an SRS deck. — [Hacking Chinese: Hiway](https://challenges.hackingchinese.com/resources/stories/379-hiway-english-chinese-language-exchange-community)

### Inferences
- The tap-a-word, save, then review loop is proven in AI-tutor apps, so users and the market understand it. It has not been joined to real-person chats in existing messengers. That combination is the gap the product idea targets.
- The incumbents' paywalls on translation (HelloTalk 10 a day, Tandem about 3 a day) suggest per-lookup cost and value matter. Word-level lookups are cheaper per call than whole-message translation, which may allow a more generous free tier. This is an inference and was not checked against costs.

### Gaps
- No primary-source confirmation of HelloTalk's word-level lookup or of Tandem having any word-level lookup.
- Talkable.chat, "Language Learning AI Chat" and Flipchat have no independent reviews, user numbers or live-status checks.

## Known user complaints: dating/creep problem, matching, retention — what does the market say is broken?

### Takeaway
Reviewers and users keep naming the same failures:
- The apps feel like dating apps: flirting, hookups, unwanted romantic and sexual approaches, worst for women and minors.
- Floods of low-quality requests, often from people who aren't native in your target language.
- Flaky partners and conversations that fizzle.
- AI corrections passed off as human ones.
- Translation and partner discovery rationed behind paywalls.

The core cause is that these are networks of strangers. That is what a product using the user's existing contacts would avoid.

### Cited Findings
- **Reddit, r/languagelearning (Aug 2024):** "Tandem seems to be a multilingual flirting app, in my experience, while HelloTalk has some serious learners (but not all)". The poster's advice: message consistently over weeks, because drawn-out conversations fizzle. — [Reddit via Redlib mirror](https://redlib.ssps.io/r/languagelearning/comments/1er5o3w/deleted_by_user/lhwf83i/?context=3)
- **Lingopie (Nov 2025)** on HelloTalk:
  - The line between learning and dating "can get pretty blurry". Voice rooms where "people are just flirting and exchanging numbers"; some users are "clearly just looking for hookups".
  - Filtering doesn't separate serious learners from the rest. Voice rooms are random, with people dropping in and out.
  - Premium add-ons are overpriced. AI corrections are indistinguishable from human ones. — [Lingopie](https://lingopie.com/blog/hellotalk-review/)
- **Speaky App Store reviews:**
  - 2017: a warning that older men DM teenage girls and "hardly ever want to talk about languages".
  - 2020: "around 1000 messages in 3 weeks", which "can be a little scary".
  - 2022: a 15-year-old reports "unwanted nudity and love messages" from adults and having to "block almost every person that messages me". — [App Store: Speaky](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1118877445)
- **Tandem App Store review (2019):** 50 requests in under an hour, "overwhelming", and "many messages from people who aren't native or fluent in my target language". The reviewer still got "fewer creepy requests than I expected". — [App Store: Tandem](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tandem-conversation-exchange/id959001619)
- **HelloTalk App Store review (2021):** profiles were "copied" and used to socialise with other people, a fake-profile or impersonation complaint. — [App Store: HelloTalk](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hellotalk-language-learning/id557130558)
- **JREF forum (undated):** a woman described unwanted romantic approaches and switched from one-to-one chats to the Moments feed. "There are a lot of creepy people out there who try to use online exchange apps selfishly." — [JREF](https://jref.com/goto/post?id=1265505)
- **Mezzo Guild Tandem review:** "a bit like a dating app (same thing is true with HelloTalk)". — [Mezzo Guild](https://www.mezzoguild.com/tandem-review/)
- **SewaYou (Apr 2026, a competitor's own blog, so biased):**
  - HelloTalk: "Some users report unwanted romantic messages", partner quality "varies enormously", and conversations feel transactional.
  - Tandem: "Same flaky-partner problem as every exchange app"; verification reduces spam but doesn't remove it. — [SewaYou blog](https://www.sewayou.com/blog/best-language-exchange-apps-2026)
- **Univext (2026, a competitor's marketing blog, so biased):** HelloTalk has more spam and a dating-app feel; Tandem has stricter verification but most useful features are behind a paywall. — [Univext HelloTalk vs Tandem 2026](https://univext.com/de/blog/360/hellotalk-vs-tandem-2026)
- **Correction quality:** another HelloTalk App Store reviewer notes that feedback from other learners can be unreliable. — [App Store: HelloTalk](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hellotalk-language-learning/id557130558)
- **Incumbents' responses:**
  - Speaky added "verification steps" against fake profiles. — [App Store: Speaky](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1118877445)
  - Tandem sells a verification badge in Pro. — [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/tandem/pricing)
  - HelloTalk VIP sells gender search and more new-partner requests. — [MacTrast](https://www.mactrast.com/?p=168083)

### Inferences
- What the market says is broken is mostly the stranger-matching layer: safety, intent mismatch, request floods and flakiness. The translation and correction tools draw milder complaints (rationing, unclear AI versus human source).
- Building on chats with existing contacts sidesteps the safety and matching complaints entirely. The cost is losing the "find a native speaker" value, which only helps users who already know speakers of their target language. The share of learners who do is unmeasured.
- Monetising safety (verification badges, gender filters) shows incumbents treat the creep problem as a revenue lever rather than solving it structurally.

### Gaps
- No quantitative retention data (D1/D30, churn) for any exchange app was found.
- No 2025–2026 Reddit threads were fetched directly. The Reddit evidence is a single 2024 comment through a mirror.
- Google Play review pages for HelloTalk and Tandem could not be fetched (the content was truncated), so Play-side complaint themes and download counts are missing.
