# AI tutors, writing-correction and tap-to-translate tools for language learners (incl. WhatsApp/Telegram delivery)

Research date: 2026-10-19. Product idea under evaluation: an app that integrates with WhatsApp, Telegram and other messengers so people can chat across languages *with real people* while learning, using tap-a-word translation and help writing their own sentence correctly. Older information is flagged with its date. Most product claims below are vendor-reported; independent usage data is rare in this category.

---

## 1. AI conversation tutors (chat-with-AI): who does written chat, who corrects and explains, and how big are they?

### Takeaway
The category's money is in voice-first AI tutors: Duolingo (12.7M paid subscribers; Video Call is its "flagship" AI feature and is being pushed into the cheaper Super tier) and Speak (>$100M annualized revenue, late 2025). Written chat with inline correction-and-explanation exists in smaller apps (Loora, Langua, Gliglish, Jumpspeak, Talkpal, Langotalk), but every one of them is a conversation with *an AI*, not with a real person. Duolingo's "Explain My Answer" explains an exercise answer, not a free sentence the learner wrote to someone.

### Cited Findings
**Duolingo (Max / Super)**
- Q2 2026 (quarter to 30 Jun 2026, released 5 Aug 2026): DAUs 58.7M (+23% YoY from 47.7M), MAUs 140.6M (+10%), paid subscribers 12.7M (+17% from 10.9M), revenue $298.5M (+18%), subscription revenue $258.0M (+22%) — [Duolingo Q2 2026 shareholder letter (SEC 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)

- Duolingo Max launched (March 2023) with Explain My Answer and Roleplay; Roleplay runs scenarios with Duolingo characters, then gives feedback on the accuracy and complexity of the learner's responses plus tips — [Duolingo blog, "Duolingo Max" (2023)](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-max)

- Langotalk: an independent language-school review (2025) lists as cons that "grammar review can sometimes be inaccurate" and robotic pronunciation; full features need a subscription — [FlexiClasses Langotalk review](https://flexiclasses.com/langotalk-review/)
- Pingo AI: scenario-based AI conversations in 25 languages, learner can invent contexts; mobile-first, no web access per one comparison — [Issen](https://www.issen.com/blog/best-ai-language-tutors-conversation-practice/); [Slova](https://getslova.com/blog/best-ai-language-tutor-apps/)

**Babbel**

### Inferences
- The two scaled businesses (Duolingo, Speak) both moved toward *spoken* AI conversation as the premium hook; written correction is becoming a free/commodity feature (Explain My Answer reportedly made free). A product centred on *written* help is therefore not competing head-on with the big tutors, but also cannot rely on "AI corrects your sentence" as a paid differentiator on its own.
- Every app in this list is a closed loop with an AI interlocutor. None documents help with messages written to a real person outside the app.
- For a Hebrew/Italian pairing: Duolingo Max-style features are documented for courses taught from English; I found no evidence that any of the big tutors supports Hebrew-speakers learning Italian with Hebrew explanations (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No public Duolingo Max subscriber count or share since Duolingo stopped breaking it out; the Q2 2026 letter refers only to Super.

- Which of these tutors support Hebrew as interface/explanation language was not verified.

## 2. Tutors living inside WhatsApp or Telegram, and Meta's 2025–2026 WhatsApp Business API AI policy

### Takeaway
Messenger-native tutors exist but are all small indie products (Zaplingo, Parlai, LangBuddy, Behappy Speak, KeelAI, Poly); the only usage figure found is Parlai's 136 paid subscriptions (Feb 2026). No Duolingo, Speak, Praktika, Pingo or Talkpal WhatsApp product was found. Meta banned general-purpose AI chatbots (AI as "the core product") from the WhatsApp Business API from 15 Jan 2026; the EU forced access back open in the EEA via interim measures on 9 Jun 2026 (Meta appealing), but the ban stands outside the EEA. Telegram has no comparable restriction found and in March 2026 shipped its own AI Editor in the message bar.

### Cited Findings
**Indie tutors on WhatsApp / Telegram**
- Zaplingo: conversation tutor on WhatsApp, Telegram, iPhone and Android; pronunciation and grammar feedback, accepts voice messages; English, Spanish, French, Italian — [The AI Report tool database](https://www.theaireport.ai/tooldatabase/zaplingo); [Callin.io](https://callin.io/ai-tools/zaplingo)
- Parlai: WhatsApp AI tutor with corrections and voice practice; free tier plus €12/month premium; 136 active subscriptions as of Feb 2026 (self-reported on a revenue-verification listing) — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/parlai)
- Behappy Speak: Telegram tutor for English and German, role-play scenarios (job interviews, travel), memory of past sessions — [Hunted.space](https://hunted.space/product/behappy-speak)
- KeelAI: Telegram-first tutor with text and voice practice plus a Telegram Mini App for flashcards and spaced repetition — [Hunted.space](https://hunted.space/dashboard/keelai)
- Poly: Telegram voice tutor claiming 17 languages and CEFR A1–C1 calibration (self-published page on an unusual domain; treat with caution) — [Poly site](https://2-linguo.duckdns.org/en/)
- LangBuddy is listed as available on WhatsApp; WhatsAI Tutor is a Product Hunt listing — [AlternativeTo LangBuddy](https://alternativeto.net/software/langbuddy/?p=2); [Product Hunt WhatsAI Tutor](https://www.producthunt.com/products/whatsai-tutor/makers)

**Meta AI inside WhatsApp as a de facto tutor**
- Spanish-language press (Aug 2024) described messaging Meta AI "Hola, quiero practicar inglés"; it proposes topics and comments on the user's level; also usable for Italian, Portuguese, French, German, Arabic, Russian and others — [Crónica (AR), Aug 11 2024](https://www.cronica.com.ar/tecnologia/WhatsApp-la-manera-en-la-que-se-puede-aprender-ingles-gracias-a-Meta-AI-20240811-0174.html); [Semana](https://www.semana.com/tecnologia/articulo/asi-puede-aprender-ingles-con-meta-ai-en-whatsapp-solo-hay-que-seguir-dos-pasos/202400/)
- 2026: Meta AI in WhatsApp began offering short Kyrgyz lessons and word explanations, while admitting its Kyrgyz is imperfect — [Akchabar](https://www.akchabar.kg/en/news/meta-ai-v-whatsapp-nachal-predlagat-polzovatelyam-uchit-kirgizskij-yazik-zsqrxnbjsxgwrmpa)

**Meta's WhatsApp Business API policy (general-purpose AI ban)**
- Applies to new API users from 15 Oct 2025 and all existing users from 15 Jan 2026; targets "AI Providers" "where the AI is the core product rather than a supporting feature"; bans open-ended/assistant-style bots (ChatGPT, Perplexity) and conversation companions; explicitly allows structured bots (support, bookings, order tracking, notifications, sales) whose role is "ancillary to a legitimate business service" — [respond.io (Oct 27 2025, updated Nov 3 2025)](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban)
- Named casualties: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Luzia, Poke; OpenAI confirmed ChatGPT would leave WhatsApp; Meta reserves the right to decide what counts as primary use — [9to5Mac, Oct 20 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/10/20/whatsapp-bans-ai-chatbots-from-using-its-business-api/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/no-more-chatgpt-and-perplexity-on-whatsapp-meta-bans-major-ai-chatbots-from-2026-1.500316016)
- Meta's stated rationale: the API is for customer support and updates, and chatbot use placed "a lot of burden" on its systems through message volume; Meta AI itself remains on WhatsApp — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meta-bans-ai-chatbots-from-whatsapp-business-api-chatgpt-to-shut-down-jan-2026-125102200335_1.html); [APH Networks](https://aphnetworks.com/news/30920-meta-says-no-more-general-purpose-chatbots-whatsapp-except-its-own)
- Italy's competition authority (AGCM) ordered Meta in Dec 2025 to suspend the terms — [Yahoo News / Reuters](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/italy-watchdog-orders-meta-halt-083455526.html)
- European Commission: formal antitrust investigation opened Dec 2025; statement of objections and threatened interim measures Feb 2026 — [Hoodline, Jun 2026](https://hoodline.com/2026/06/brussels-smacks-meta-orders-whatsapp-to-reopen-to-rival-bots/); [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/eu-orders-meta-to-reopen-whatsapp-to-rival-ai-chatbots-32a42eda)
- March 2026: Meta offered to let rival AI chatbots onto WhatsApp in Europe, but for a fee; the Commission said the fee was equivalent to the ban — [TechCrunch, Mar 5 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/meta-will-allow-rival-ai-chatbots-on-whatsapp-in-europe-but-for-a-fee/); [AP via NY1, Jun 9 2026](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/ap-top-news/2026/06/09/eu-orders-meta-to-restore-whatsapp-access-for-rival-ai-chatbots)
- 9 Jun 2026 interim measures: restore access on pre-15-Oct-2025 terms within five working days, in force until the investigation ends or June 2029; non-compliance fines up to 10% of turnover — [Morningstar / Dow Jones](https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202606095215/meta-gets-eu-antitrust-order-to-open-whatsapp-to-rival-ai-chatbots); [AP via NY1](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/ap-top-news/2026/06/09/eu-orders-meta-to-restore-whatsapp-access-for-rival-ai-chatbots)
- Meta called it "regulatory overreach" and said "We will appeal" — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/370580/eu-orders-meta-open-whatsapp-rival-ai-chatbots)
- ChatGPT reportedly returned to WhatsApp across the EEA, active as of 13 Jul 2026, and Meta's appeal does not suspend compliance (single source) — [TechTimes, Jul 15 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320553/20260715/eu-antitrust-order-reopens-whatsapp-chatgpt-setting-precedent-ai-markets.htm)

**Telegram**
- 31 Mar 2026 Telegram added an AI Editor in the message bar that can "fix grammar, rewrite text in a different style, or translate a message into another language"; styles include Formal, Short, Tribal, Corp, Zen, Biblical, Viking; it appears after typing more than 3 lines; powered by Telegram's Cocoon AI with requests processed "in a confidential environment" — [Telegram blog, Mar 31 2026](https://telegram.org/blog/ai-editor-mighty-polls-and-more)
- A beta review says free use is capped and Premium raises the limit roughly 50x (not confirmed officially) — [Pasquale Pillitteri guide (2026)](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/697/telegram-ai-editor-cocoon-guide)

### Inferences
- A WhatsApp Business API bot is a *separate chat with a bot*, not a layer inside the learner's conversation with a friend. Outside the EEA (e.g. Israel), a bot whose AI tutoring *is* the product plausibly falls under Meta's "AI is the core product" definition and could be removed at Meta's discretion; inside the EEA (including Italy) the interim order currently protects access. Indie tutors like Parlai and Zaplingo were still listed in 2026, so enforcement against small tutors may be lax, but that is a platform risk, not a right.
- Telegram is the friendlier bot platform (bots in groups, Mini Apps for SRS as KeelAI shows), but Telegram now ships its own free grammar-fix/translate layer, so a Telegram product needs to add *learning* (explanations, saved words), not just correction.
- Monetisation in this niche is small: freemium with ~€10–12/month premium; the only number found (136 subscriptions) suggests indie scale.

### Gaps
- No user counts for Zaplingo, LangBuddy, Behappy Speak, KeelAI or Poly; Pingo and Talkpal WhatsApp offerings were not found and may not exist.
- No Duolingo WhatsApp tutor or experiment was found in searches (absence of evidence, not proof); messenger tutors found all follow the chat + voice + corrections + freemium pattern, with no independent usage data.
- Whether Meta has acted against any language-tutor bot specifically under the AI Provider clause: no evidence found either way.
- Status of Meta's appeal of the June 2026 order as of Oct 2026 is not confirmed beyond the single TechTimes report.
- Telegram Bot API terms on AI bots were not checked; Telegram AI Editor's Premium gating is unconfirmed officially.
- Quality of Meta AI as a tutor (does it explain grammar rules?) — only 2024 press how-tos found.

## 3. Writing-correction tools: who explains the rule vs silently fixes, and do they support Italian and Hebrew?

### Takeaway
Italian is well covered (Grammarly, LanguageTool, DeepL Write, partly QuillBot); Hebrew is covered by none of the dedicated writing checkers found. The mainstream tools are built to *fix* writing for productivity (rewrites, tone, red/blue underlines), not to teach: Grammarly's language page and DeepL Write's positioning describe corrections and rewrites, not grammar-rule explanations. General LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini) are the de facto explainer for learners, with small-study evidence that explanation quality varies with prompt language.

### Cited Findings
- Grammarly: writing support listed for ~23–28 languages including Italian; Hebrew not listed. Spelling/basic grammar in all listed languages; advanced grammar, clarity, fluency, tone and paragraph rewrites (paid) only in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian; in-line translation is paid; free users get 3 advanced ("blue") suggestions per day; the page does not mention grammar-rule explanations — [Grammarly languages page (2026)](https://www.grammarly.com/languages); [Grammarly support](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000090971-Does-Grammarly-support-languages-other-than-English)
- LanguageTool: ~30 languages; the official free/premium language list and the rules page (dated 2025-03-27) do not include Hebrew; users have requested Hebrew and maintainers say new languages rely on community contributors — [LanguageTool languages](https://languagetool.org/languages); [LanguageTool help center](https://help.languagetool.org/hc/en-us/articles/39254526141463); [LanguageTool forum, "Please add support for Hebrew"](https://forum.languagetool.org/t/please-add-support-for-hebrew/7703)
- DeepL Write: per DeepL's blog, nine languages as of 2026 — English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Korean; no Hebrew — [DeepL blog](https://www.deepl.com/blog/deepl-write-welcomes-french-spanish); described as a monolingual rewriting tool rather than a translator (Aug 2026) — [Flowith blog](https://flowith.io/blog/deepl-write-vs-grammarly-writing-assistant-non-native-speakers/). A third-party claim of "30+ languages" contradicts DeepL's own list — [AI Indigo](https://aiindigo.com/tool/deepl-write). DeepL's help centre has an article titled "DeepL for WhatsApp Web" (translation, not Write) — [DeepL support](https://support.deepl.com/hc/en-us/articles/7786508619420-DeepL-for-WhatsApp-Web)
- QuillBot: Chrome Web Store listing says writing/editing in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Dutch (no Italian, no Hebrew); a Scribbr comparison says the grammar checker covers 6 languages while the paraphraser covers 30+ — [Chrome Web Store, QuillBot](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/iidnbdjijdkbmajdffnidomddglmieko); [Scribbr, QuillBot vs LanguageTool](https://www.scribbr.com/?p=713641)
- Telegram's AI Editor and WhatsApp-adjacent keyboards fix or rewrite text without a teaching layer (see Q5) — [Telegram blog](https://telegram.org/blog/ai-editor-mighty-polls-and-more)
- ChatGPT as grammar explainer: a 2025 VNU (Vietnam) study found ChatGPT's grammar explanations varied with the prompt language and with whether the prompt emphasised exam rules or communicative meaning — [VNU Journal of Foreign Studies](https://jfs.ulis.vnu.edu.vn/index.php/fs/article/download/5485/4632/14680); a 2026 study of GPT-4o grammar explanations for Indonesian learners notes systematic reviews find perception studies dominate and evidence of instructional effectiveness is scarce — [Mirlam journal, 2026](https://ojs3.unpatti.ac.id/index.php/mirlam/article/download/27502/13277)

### Inferences
- For Hebrew-speaking learners writing Italian, Italian-side checking exists (Grammarly, LanguageTool, DeepL Write), but *explanations in Hebrew* are not offered by any dedicated checker found; only a general LLM would do that today. That is a real gap for a Hebrew-UI product.
- The "silently fix" design of mainstream tools is a feature for professionals and a bug for learners (the user sends a corrected message without learning why). An explain-then-send flow is a differentiator only if it is fast enough not to slow down a live chat.

### Gaps
- Microsoft Editor's 2025–2026 status (retired into Copilot or not) and language list: not found in this pass.
- Whether Grammarly's per-suggestion explanation cards exist in Italian, and whether LanguageTool's per-rule messages are shown in the learner's language: not verified.
- Gemini-specific evidence as a language tool: not found.
- DeepL's official Write support page returned HTTP 403; the language list comes from DeepL's blog.

## 4. Reading tools with word-level tap-and-save (comprehensible input): do any extend to chat apps?

### Takeaway
LingQ, Language Reactor, Readlang, Toucan and Migaku all provide word-level lookup tied to saving/flashcards, but on articles, imported text and streaming video; none documents support for WhatsApp or Telegram chats. WhatsApp Web is served only by generic translator extensions (no vocabulary/SRS), and Android overlay/accessibility apps translate chats but keep flashcards separate.

### Cited Findings
- LingQ: import text, audio or YouTube; word-level glosses and spaced repetition; 30+ languages; free with paid tier ~$12.99/month — [comprehensibleinput.it.com (2026)](https://comprehensibleinput.it.com/blog/best-comprehensible-input-apps-2026)
- Language Reactor: browser extension for Netflix and YouTube with dual subtitles, dictionary popups and phrase flashcards; 100+ languages; free plus pro ~$10/month — [comprehensibleinput.it.com](https://comprehensibleinput.it.com/blog/best-comprehensible-input-apps-2026); [Wikipedia, Language Reactor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_Reactor). Sources conflict on whether it works on arbitrary websites — [Lingopie](https://lingopie.com/blog/best-chrome-extensions-for-language-learning/)
- Migaku: Japanese-focused lookups, furigana and sentence mining on browser content and streaming sites; ~$9/month — [comprehensibleinput.it.com](https://comprehensibleinput.it.com/blog/best-comprehensible-input-apps-2026); [Yomimaru, Migaku alternatives](https://yomimaru.app/learn/migaku-alternative)
- Readlang: translates words and phrases live on any web page and turns them into flashcards — [Vocabo, best extensions 2026](https://www.vocabo.io/best-language-learning-extensions); [Lingopie](https://lingopie.com/blog/best-chrome-extensions-for-language-learning/)
- Toucan: works on regular web pages, owned by Babbel since 2023 and free — [Lingopie](https://lingopie.com/blog/best-chrome-extensions-for-language-learning/)
- LingQ users have asked for Migaku/Language Reactor-style in-place features, i.e. LingQ's strength is its own reader, not overlaying other sites — [LingQ forum](https://forum.lingq.com/t/bringing-migakulanguagereactor-style-features-to-lingq/2036672)
- WhatsApp Web has a cluster of generic translator extensions (no learning layer) — [Chrome Web Store, WhatsApp Translator](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/whatsapp-translator/anhmcblpecaabfbedccbofmjodfdjcdj?hl=en); [Kaption AI blog](https://kaptionai.com/blog/en/whatsapp-web-translator-extension-real-time/)
- Android: "Translator for WhatsApp (LangLang)" auto-translates every message using Android's AccessibilityService API — [Google Play, LangLang](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.langlang.translator_for_wsp&hl=en_IN); "Instant Translate On Screen" puts a dot in any app's input box to translate before sending, with a flashcard feature separate from chat — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spaceship.screen.textcopy&hl=en)
- Save-and-review apps such as WordPlus turn every translation into a flashcard but do not read chats — [WordPlus](https://www.wordplus.app/en)
- User-count figures for LingQ, Language Reactor, Migaku and Readlang were not found in public sources — [comprehensibleinput.it.com](https://comprehensibleinput.it.com/blog/best-comprehensible-input-apps-2026)

### Inferences
- The "tap a word, save it, review it" loop is mature for *media* input but absent for *messages from people you know*, which are arguably the most personally relevant comprehensible input a learner gets.
- Technically, reaching WhatsApp messages means either WhatsApp Web DOM extensions (desktop only), Android accessibility/overlay services (privacy-sensitive, Android only, and store-review risk), keyboards (see only the user's own draft, not the partner's reply), or copy/share-sheet into a separate app. Readlang/Toucan's "any website" claims might work on WhatsApp Web but this is untested.

### Gaps
- Lingvist not covered in this pass.
- Whether Readlang, Toucan or Migaku actually function on web.whatsapp.com or web.telegram.org: no source; needs a hands-on test.
- No user counts for any reader tool.

## 5. Who combines a real conversation with a real person + AI help in the moment (draft check before sending, tap words in the reply)?

### Takeaway
Partial combinations exist in three forms, but none found does all of: (1) inside the messengers people already use, (2) a pre-send check that *explains* the mistake, and (3) tap-a-word in the partner's reply that feeds vocabulary review. Language-exchange apps (HelloTalk, Tandem) do 2 and partly 3, but only inside their own network of strangers. Tokki does 1-ish and 3 (friends, tap word, per-chat vocabulary) as its own standalone messenger, with no AI writing help and ~300 users. The platforms themselves (WhatsApp translation, Telegram AI Editor, Apple Live Translation, Galaxy AI) do translate/fix with no teaching layer.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The white space the product idea targets is real as of Oct 2026: no product found brings *explained* pre-send correction plus tap-a-word-to-SRS into chats the learner already has on WhatsApp/Telegram. The nearest analogues each miss a leg: HelloTalk/Tandem (strangers, own app), Tokki (own messenger, no AI writing help, tiny), keyboards (fix without teaching, can't see the reply), platform features (translate/fix for everyone, not for learners).
- The threat is platform absorption: WhatsApp (translation), Telegram (AI Editor) and Apple/Samsung (OS-level translation) already give away the "understand / fix" half for free. The defensible part is the learning loop (why it was wrong, words saved from real conversations, review), plus language pairs the platforms underserve (e.g. Hebrew explanations).
- Integration reality shapes the design: a keyboard can do the draft check on both iOS and Android but cannot read the partner's reply; tapping words in replies needs Android accessibility/overlay, WhatsApp Web extensions, a Telegram bot/Mini App, or copy/share into the app. A WhatsApp Business bot cannot sit inside a user-to-user chat and carries the AI-provider policy risk outside the EEA (Q2).
- Tokki's substitution mechanic (learned words swapped into friends' messages) is a notable precedent for "learning from your own chats" and shows that friend-chat learning is being tried, but at ~300 users there is no evidence yet of demand at scale.
